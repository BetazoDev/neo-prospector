# Design System: Hemeron Prospector

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Living Field"**

Every business the team finds is a dot, and the app is a search box laid over the field of all those dots. The field fills as sites are checked: crimson where a redesign is due, grey where the site is fine, a pulsing ring while a check runs. Everything else in the system exists to frame that field and get the seller from a crimson dot to a WhatsApp conversation.

The world is hemeron.com's, put to work. Carbon fields hold the shell and every page header. Niebla fields sit under the lists you read, and paper panels carry the rows and the business file. One crimson carries every signal that matters. Display type is Clash Grotesk at 500 with a dimmed second line. Everything you read is Switzer with tabular figures. Density follows the job: page heads are calm and spacious, the lead list is dense and scannable, and on a phone it collapses to a stacked card per business.

It rejects the default admin look: no icon sidebar, no grid of stat cards, no spreadsheet of undifferentiated rows. Counts live in a thin stage rail and in large crimson numerals next to each base's field.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two working fields: carbon (`#0e0e0e`) for frame and headers, niebla (`#ecebeb`) with paper panels for reading.
- One accent, crimson, used for big numerals, the primary action and dots, never for small text.
- Pills everywhere you click. Panels at 18px radius, fields at 12px.
- Flat surfaces. Shadows appear only on things that float above the page.
- The dot field is the signature, and it is live and linked.

## Colors

A near-monochrome carbon and paper palette with a single crimson that marks opportunity and action.

### Primary
- **Hemeron Crimson** (`{colors.crimson}`): the only accent. Used for large numerals (candidate counts on base rows, high scores, the drawer score), the primary action (Buscar negocios, the drawer's WhatsApp), candidate and demo dots, live and state dots, the current stage underline in the rail, focus rings, selection and caret. Hover deepens to **Pressed Crimson** (`{colors.crimson-hover}`). Press goes to **Vino** (`{colors.vino}`). **Crimson Wash** (`{colors.crimson-soft}`) is only for the focus halo, busy pills and the error box. The active lead row tints to **Blush** (`{colors.row-active}`). **White** (`{colors.on-crimson}`) is the text on every crimson fill (primary buttons, the drawer WhatsApp, text selection). It is also the hover lift of a lead row on paper.

### Neutral
- **Carbon** (`{colors.carbon}`): the shell, top bar, stage rail, page heads, drawer head, login.
- **Carbon 2 / 3 / 4** (`{colors.carbon-2}`, `{colors.carbon-3}`, `{colors.carbon-4}`): raised dark surfaces in steps: inputs, the base field panel, popovers and the palette, then hover and current nav, then progress tracks.
- **Dark Rules** (`{colors.line-dark}`, `{colors.line-dark-strong}`): dividers on carbon, then outlines on dark pills and fields.
- **Niebla** (`{colors.niebla}`): the work field under lead lists.
- **Paper** (`{colors.paper}`): the lead list panel, the drawer body, light chips and pills, tooltips, and the light ink on dark (`{colors.on-dark}` has the same value).
- **Light Rules** (`{colors.line-light}`, `{colors.line-light-strong}`): row dividers on paper, then outlines on light pills.
- **Ink on dark** (`{colors.on-dark}` / `{colors.on-dark-2}` / `{colors.on-dark-3}`): primary, secondary and meta text on carbon, all AA on carbon and carbon-2. **Dim Ink** (`{colors.on-dark-dim}`) is only for the second line of display titles, zero counts and separators.
- **Ink on paper** (`{colors.ink}` / `{colors.ink-2}` / `{colors.ink-3}`): primary, secondary and meta text on niebla and paper.

### State colors (dots and chips only)
- **Amber** (`{colors.amber}`, with `{colors.amber-ink}` on light): manual review.
- **Good Green** (`{colors.good}`): finished steps and success toasts on dark. On light fields the success chip uses **Forest Ink** (`{colors.good-ink}`) for text and `{colors.good-ink-rule}` for its outline, over a green wash.
- **Rating Gold** (`{colors.rating-star}`): the Google rating star on light fields (list and drawer), and nothing else.
- **Fine Grey** (`{colors.dot-fine}`): a site that was checked and is acceptable. Note: the brief named aluminio for this. The build uses this darker grey, and aluminio (`#d7d8d7`) is defined but never applied.
- Not a color: the opaque `#000` stop in the stage rail's right-edge fade is a mask-image alpha stop (it sets full opacity, it never paints).
- **Pending** (`{colors.dot-pending}`) and **Discarded** (`{colors.dot-discarded}`): dots not yet checked, and dots retired from the base.

### Named Rules
**The One Crimson Rule.** `#ce0037` is the only accent. Its lighter pink sibling (`#ff5c80`) is retired. If something needs to stand out and it is not a candidate, a high score, the primary action or a live signal, it does not get crimson.

**The Big Crimson Rule.** Crimson text appears only at numeral size (20px and up). Small labels stay in ink, and their state is shown by a crimson dot or icon placed next to them.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Clash Grotesk 500 (with Switzer, system sans)
**Body Font:** Switzer 400/500/600 (with system sans)

**Character:** A tight, confident grotesk for titles and numerals over a neutral, highly legible sans for everything you read. Both are hemeron.com's own faces, loaded from Fontshare. Figures are tabular everywhere.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (`{typography.display}`): the home question ("¿Qué negocios / buscamos hoy?"). Under 760px it is fixed at `{typography.display-mobile}`. The second line is dimmed.
- **Headline** (`{typography.headline}`): page titles on carbon heads (base name, Embudo, Bases). They also get the dimmed second line.
- **Title** (`{typography.title}`): section titles. `{typography.title-drawer}` is the drawer business name. `{typography.title-sm}` is for base-row names and empty states. `{typography.title-xs}` is for drawer section heads. The Bases group heading uses the title face at 20px.
- **Numeral** (Clash 500, tabular): `{typography.numeral-xl}` is the drawer score. `{typography.numeral}` is base-row candidate counts, which drop to 26px under 760px. `{typography.numeral-md}` is lead-list scores. `{typography.numeral-sm}` is stage-rail counts. `{typography.numeral-xs}` is menu counts (recent bases). Legend counts are 15px.
- **Lede** (`{typography.lede}`): the Inicio lede (max 46ch). The same 17px is used for the value typed in the home box fields and in the palette input.
- **Body** (`{typography.body}`): lead names and reading text.
- **Body UI** (`{typography.body-ui}`): working UI text, including menu and palette rows, drawer key-value rows and findings, run steps, status lines, the page meta row, dark inputs and the drawer WhatsApp.
- **Body Small** (`{typography.body-sm}`): meta lines, light-field buttons, WhatsApp pills, legend items, toolbar notes.
- **Mobile input floor** (`{typography.input-mobile}`): under 760px every text input, select and textarea is forced to 16px so iOS does not zoom on focus.
- **Label** (`{typography.label}`): uppercase, tracked. Used for meta, legends, column heads, the product name, stage names and tooltip state. `{typography.label-xs}` is only for the mobile bottom-nav labels, which sit under 20px icons.

### Named Rules
**The Dimmed Second Line Rule.** Display and headline titles split into two lines. The second line is a quieter restatement in dim ink (`{colors.on-dark-dim}`), never a separate kicker above the title.

**The Tracked-Is-Meta Rule.** Tracked uppercase is reserved for meta, legends, column heads, navigation and the pill buttons on the carbon field (`{typography.button-tracked}`). Working buttons on the light field, row actions and WhatsApp pills are sentence case.

## Layout

The page is centered at a 1440px max width with 40px gutters (16px under 760px). A sticky carbon head stacks a 64px top bar over a 44px stage rail. The rail scrolls sideways and fades at its right edge under 1240px. Screens follow one pattern: a carbon page head (crumbs, two-line headline, meta row joined with "·", actions, and on a base a status line plus a field panel with a filtering legend), then a niebla work field holding a toolbar and the paper lead list.

Home is a two-column grid at 11fr / 13fr with a 72px gap (48px under 1240px). It stacks into one column under 960px. The lead list is a seven-column grid: score, business, site, why, contact, Google, more. The Google column drops under 1180px. Under 900px each row becomes a card, with the score in a left column and name, site, why and contact stacked beside it. Under 760px a fixed four-item bottom nav (62px plus the safe area) replaces the top nav, and the drawer goes full width.

Spacing follows a 4px base (4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32), with larger steps for page heads (40/32px) and home (64/80px).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from the field change: carbon frame, niebla field, paper panel. Each surface is flat at rest, with 1px rules. Shadows appear only on layers that float above the page. They are always dark, soft and offset downward, or leftward for the drawer.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Popover** (`box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0,0,0,0.7)`): menus off the top bar.
- **Palette** (`box-shadow: 0 32px 80px -16px rgba(0,0,0,0.8)`): the Cmd/Ctrl+K search.
- **Drawer** (`box-shadow: -24px 0 60px -20px rgba(0,0,0,0.45)`): the business file sliding in from the right.
- **Light menu** (`box-shadow: 0 18px 40px -12px rgba(0,0,0,0.25)`): row menus on paper.
- **Tooltip and toast** (`0 10px 24px -6px rgba(0,0,0,0.55)`, `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`).
- **Focus halo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px {colors.crimson-soft}`): fields on focus, with the border turning crimson.

### Named Rules
**The Fields-Not-Shadows Rule.** To separate two regions, change the field (carbon, niebla, paper), not the shadow. Shadows are only for layers that float.

## Shapes

Everything you press is a full pill (`{rounded.pill}`): buttons, nav items, chips, legend items, selects, the search trigger, WhatsApp. Dots and the account button are circles. Thin 3px progress bars (the status bar under a base header and the activity bar in the activity menu) use `{rounded.bar}` so their ends are fully round. Containers use generous soft corners, stepped by size: menu items 10px, inputs 12px, base rows on hover 14px, the run card 16px, the lead list and base field 18px, login and the palette 20px. Borders are 1px hairlines, and dashed lines appear only in the empty-bases state.

## Components

### Buttons
Rounded, quiet and confident: one crimson pill per view, everything else outlined.
- **Shape:** full pill (`{rounded.pill}`), 40px tall. Small is 32px, large is 52px.
- **Primary:** crimson with white tracked uppercase (`{components.button-primary}`). Hover is `{colors.crimson-hover}`, press is `{colors.vino}`, and every press scales to 0.97.
- **Ghost (carbon field):** transparent with a `{colors.line-dark-strong}` outline and tracked uppercase. Hover fills `{colors.carbon-3}`.
- **Light (niebla/paper field):** paper with a `{colors.line-light-strong}` outline, ink text, sentence case at 13px. Hover darkens the outline to `{colors.ink-3}`.
- **Icon button:** 36px circle, outlined on dark or light. The `quiet` variant has no outline.
- **Focus:** 2px crimson outline, 2px offset.

### WhatsApp pill
This control carries the proof behind each number.
- **Confirmed** (the number is published on the business's own site): a filled ink pill reading "WhatsApp" (`{components.whatsapp-confirmed}`).
- **Probe** (a Maps phone that may not have WhatsApp): an outlined pill reading "Probar WhatsApp" (`{components.whatsapp-probe}`).
- **Drawer primary:** crimson, 44px (`{components.whatsapp-primary}`). It goes full width on mobile.
- All three are sentence case, with a 15px message icon.

### Chips
- **Style:** 24px pill with a 1px outline, 12px medium. Outlined on carbon. On light fields they get a paper fill and `{colors.ink-2}`.
- **Tones:** accent, warn (amber), good (green) and muted. Tone comes from the stage or site status. On carbon, the accent chip has on-dark text over a crimson outline and wash. On light fields it has ink text with a crimson outline and a faint crimson wash, never crimson text.

### Legend (filters the field)
- Each 34px pill shows a state's dot, its name and its count as a numeral. Selected (`aria-pressed`) turns it into a solid paper pill with ink text. Static legends do not hover. It scrolls sideways on mobile.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Dark input:** `{colors.carbon-2}` with a `{colors.line-dark-strong}` outline, 12px radius, 40px.
- **Box field:** the 62px home field with its label inside, 17px input text.
- **Light search:** a paper pill with an inset search icon. The select is a paper pill with an inline chevron.
- **Focus:** the border turns crimson and a 3px crimson-wash halo appears. The caret is crimson. Inputs are 16px on mobile to avoid zoom.

### Navigation
- **Top bar:** the Hemeron wordmark and "PROSPECTOR" label, then tracked uppercase nav pills. The current item fills `{colors.carbon-3}`. Then the Cmd+K search trigger with a kbd hint, the activity pill (crimson-tinted with a beating dot while busy) and the round account button.
- **Stage rail:** Clash counts beside tracked stage names, divided by hairlines. A stage with candidates gets a 7px crimson dot. The current stage gets a 2px crimson underline. Zero counts use dim ink.
- **Mobile:** a fixed four-item bottom bar with 20px icons and 10px tracked labels. The current item's icon is crimson.

### Lead list and drawer
- **Progress bars:** 3px tracks in `{colors.carbon-4}` with a crimson fill that grows by transform (500ms).
- **Lead list:** a paper panel (`{rounded.panel}`) on niebla. Rows are 76px minimum with hairline dividers. Hover goes white, the active row is blush, and muted rows drop to 50% opacity. Each row leads with its dot and score numeral, crimson when the score is high.
- **Drawer (business file):** 520px on the right, full width on mobile. It has a carbon head with prev/next, a 48px score numeral and a Clash name, then a paper body of findings (weighted, worst first; heavy findings set their weight in ink at 600, not crimson), key-value facts and notes, then a paper foot with the WhatsApp primary. J/K steps through the businesses in field order.

### Dot field (signature)
The base, the home rows and the empty state all share one component.
- **Grammar:** one dot per business, carried as one letter per state (`c` candidate, `w` preparing demo, `d` demo ready, `k` contacted, `u` manual review, `o` acceptable site, `n` no own site, `r` checking now, `p` pending, `x` discarded). The model lives in `lib/pipeline/dots.ts`.
- **Rendering:** dots are 8px with a 5px gap (small 6/4, large 11/6). Candidate is solid crimson. Preparing demo is a crimson ring that breathes. Demo ready is crimson with a paper inner ring. Contacted is paper. Manual review is amber. Acceptable is fine grey. No own site is a hollow grey ring. Checking now is paper and pulses. Pending is `{colors.dot-pending}`. Discarded is `{colors.dot-discarded}`.
- **Behavior:** the field fills in place as checks land, and colors cross-fade over 600ms. Hovering a row lights its dot, scaled 1.6 with a carbon-and-ink ring. Hovering a dot shows a paper tooltip with the name and state, and clicking a dot opens that business. The legend dims non-matching dots to 18%. Reduced motion stops the pulses.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** frame every screen with a carbon head and put lists on a niebla field inside a paper panel.
- **Do** show opportunity as a crimson numeral (20px and up) or a crimson dot. Keep the label beside it in ink.
- **Do** use one crimson primary per view (Buscar negocios, Nueva búsqueda, Revisar sus sitios, the drawer's WhatsApp). Every other action is a ghost or light pill.
- **Do** keep tracked uppercase for meta, legends, column heads, nav and the dark-field pill buttons. Use sentence case for light-field buttons, row actions and WhatsApp.
- **Do** distinguish a confirmed WhatsApp (filled ink "WhatsApp") from a Maps-phone probe (outlined "Probar WhatsApp").
- **Do** draw any new collection of businesses as a dot field using the shared state letters, linked both ways with its list.
- **Do** use the 1440px page, 40/16px gutters and the 4px spacing steps.

### Don't:
- **Don't** set small text (labels, chips, menu items, weights) in crimson. Use ink text with a crimson dot or icon.
- **Don't** use `#ff5c80` or any second pink or red tint as an accent.
- **Don't** add an icon sidebar, a grid of stat cards or a plain spreadsheet table. Counts go in the stage rail and beside the dot field.
- **Don't** separate regions with shadows. Change the field instead, and keep shadows for layers that float.
- **Don't** use square or slightly rounded buttons. Everything you press is a pill.
